Shabpa / Backup and clean-up

Folders safe, disk light

Shabpa backs up the folders you choose on a schedule and verifies the copies. It also shows what eats your disk and finds duplicate files, without deleting anything behind your back.

Download Shabpa

How do I back up folders and free up disk space?

  1. Open "Files and backup" from the side menu.
  2. On the backup tab choose the folders and the destination and set a schedule. Copies are verified once they are made.
  3. Use "Space analysis" to see the biggest files and folders and "Duplicates" to find extra copies.
  4. Use "Clean-up" to see temp and cache files; the size is shown before deleting and it asks for confirmation.

Restore never overwrites your current files.

Safety

  • Scheduled backup with verification of the copies
  • A restore that never overwrites current files
  • Anything deleted goes to the Recycle Bin only, after a preview and only from the folder you scanned
  • Clean-up touches temporary and cache folders only

Frequently asked questions

Does Shabpa delete my files?

Only after a preview and your confirmation, and only to the Recycle Bin. Files are not erased for good, and work happens only in the folder you scanned.

Is cleaning temp files safe?

Yes. It only cleans temporary and cache folders, shows how much it will free before deleting and asks for confirmation.
