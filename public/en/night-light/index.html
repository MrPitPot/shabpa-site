Shabpa / Night light

Go easy on your eyes at night, dimmer than your monitor allows

Some monitors are too bright at night even on their lowest setting. Shabpa dims the screen below the monitor's own minimum and applies a warm night tint the way Windows' Night Light does, without turning the picture yellow-grey.

Download Shabpa

How do I dim the screen below the minimum?

  1. Open "Night light and rest" from the side menu.
  2. Switch dimming on and set the darkness with the slider; it goes below the monitor's minimum.
  3. Turn on the warm night tint with one of the color-temperature (Kelvin) presets and adjust it if you like.
  4. Set your night hours so dimming starts automatically every evening. Keyboard shortcuts also toggle it and dim or brighten.

For late nights there is also a sleep mode and break reminders.

What you get

  • Night light with adjustable color temperature
  • Auto-dim during your night hours
  • Keyboard shortcuts to toggle and to dim or brighten
  • Break reminders and sleep mode
  • A microphone level test

Frequently asked questions

How is it different from Windows' Night Light?

Windows' Night Light only warms the color. Shabpa can also dim the screen below the monitor's own minimum, and its warm tint works like Windows' Night Light and never turns the picture yellow-grey.

Can it damage my monitor?

No. Darkness and tint are applied to the picture output and never change the monitor's hardware settings; closing or switching off the app puts everything back.
