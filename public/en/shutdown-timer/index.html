Shabpa / Shutdown timer

Shut your PC down automatically, on time and without surprises

If you fall asleep during a movie, a download or a game and the PC runs until morning, you need a good shutdown timer. Shabpa schedules shutdown, sleep, hibernate and restart, and shows a countdown before each one that you can cancel at any moment.

Download Shabpa

How do I shut down my PC automatically?

  1. Open Shabpa, choose "System control" from the side menu and then the "Power command" tab.
  2. Pick the action: shut down, sleep, hibernate or restart.
  3. Set the time: right now, in a few minutes or hours, or at an exact time. For every night, create a repeating schedule (say, every night at 1:30).
  4. Confirm. A card shows the exact time it will run, and a countdown appears on screen before the shutdown.

A faster way: press Ctrl+Alt+Space and type "shut down at 1 am" or "sleep in an hour and a quarter". Shabpa's command box works fully offline.

What an ordinary timer does not give you

  • Conditional shutdown: when a download or upload finishes, the PC goes idle, a program closes or a heavy task ends
  • Wake timers to bring the PC back from sleep
  • Sleep and wake history and power use
  • A late command never runs: if the PC was off or asleep at the scheduled time, it will not shut down by surprise later
  • Every schedule shows its exact time and asks for confirmation before it is saved

Frequently asked questions

What if it shuts down in the middle of my work?

A countdown appears on screen first and you can cancel it. The length of the countdown is known in advance.

Can it shut down after a download finishes?

Yes. In "Conditional shutdown" choose "when the download finishes"; once network use drops, the PC shuts down with the same countdown.
