Shabpa / Updates

Update your apps, graphics driver and Windows in one place

Old programs and drivers lower your security and make games run worse. Shabpa shows what is out of date, but never installs anything without your approval.

Download Shabpa

How do I check for app and driver updates?

  1. Open "Health and security" from the side menu.
  2. Choose the "App updates" tab and press "Check for updates". Shabpa asks Windows' own package manager (winget) which programs have a newer version.
  3. Select the programs you want to update and confirm; they are downloaded from the publisher and checked.
  4. In the "Drivers" tab you see your graphics driver version and open the maker's official page (AMD, NVIDIA or Intel). "Search" also finds drivers that Windows Update has approved for your hardware.

Windows update and service status are checked in the "System health" tab of the same section.

What you get

  • Outdated programs through winget; only versions that fit your PC are offered
  • Installation only after you confirm; Windows may ask for administrator approval for some, and open programs may be closed
  • Installed graphics driver version and a link to the maker's official page
  • Driver suggestions from Windows Update
  • A check of Windows update and service status

Frequently asked questions

Does Shabpa update my programs on its own?

No, nothing is installed without your approval. Shabpa only shows the list and, after you choose and confirm, installs through winget from the publisher's site.

How does Shabpa update itself?

The app fetches the new version from several sources (GitHub, this site and an Iranian host), checks the digital signature of the update manifest, and runs the file only if its SHA-256 matches the published value.
