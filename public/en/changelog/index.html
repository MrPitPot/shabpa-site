Changelog

What every Shabpa version added and fixed, short and plain. The full technical list is on GitHub. CHANGELOG.md

3.16.0 · 2026-11-06

What's new

  • Rate Shabpa, and optionally show your rating on the website
  • Lighting: one rainbow in step on every device, richer colours and 8 new effects

Fixes

  • The game overlay preview is no longer cut off at the bottom

3.15.1 · 2026-11-06

Fixes

  • Fixed the ping stability page error when another program holds the speed-limit driver
  • Fixed the overlay suddenly jumping to another monitor
  • Better animation for the premium themes

3.15.0 · 2026-11-03

What's new

  • Reminders work like an alarm clock, with a sound until you close them
  • Switch off any part of the app you do not want, in Settings
  • Save a problem report to the desktop to send to us
  • Feathers are collected 30% faster
  • Updates are no longer forced; you are only told a new version is out

Fixes

  • Lighting starts by itself after a restart or boot
  • Lighting: switching it off and on and finding devices work better
  • Speed limits no longer cut out on every Alt+Tab
  • The lighting devices window is easier to read
  • The seven-night gift was not paid twice any more

3.14.3 · 2026-11-03

Fixes

  • Lighting now also turns on on networks where its download site is blocked

3.14.2 · 2026-11-02

Fixes

  • If lighting did not work on your PC (especially Windows 10), the app now says why and shows the right fix

3.14.1 · 2026-11-02

Fixes

  • App speed limits work again after an update
  • An install or update no longer stops half way
  • The app window freezes less
  • After install, the Shabpa window comes to the front

3.14.0 · 2026-11-02

What's new

  • Boofi gets dressed: a feather shop with 90 items and a «My Boofi» page
  • 30 premium themes with moving backgrounds, bought with feathers
  • RGB lighting inside Shabpa with one click, with case and desk preview, a devices window, a synced rainbow, on/off effects and over 40 flowing effects
  • Boofi: 61 jokes and 57 facts, a password maker, unit converter, world clocks and more
  • 200 gift feathers for entering your name and birthday; 3 feathers for every hour with Shabpa
  • The programs that use the most memory, on the memory page
  • Automatic updates (signed versions only)

Fixes

  • Switching RGB off sometimes did not finish
  • Some lights (memory, motherboard) ran up to a second behind the rest
  • The borderless setting in game tuning no longer wipes your other graphics settings
  • Shabpa's graphics use went from 5–15% to about 2%

Everything stays on this PC.

3.13.0 · 2026-10-31

What's new

  • Medals, feathers and levels: everything you do with Shabpa earns feathers and medals and grows Boofi up to level 100
  • 82 medals from bronze to legendary and 8 secret ones; daily and weekly quests and streaks
  • A small notice for each new medal, never in the middle of a game
  • Save any medal as a story picture with 10 designs
  • Boofi answers in the language you write in, with about 60 new jokes, facts, tips and a calculator

Fixes

  • The game tuning step in the tour no longer blinks

All medals and feathers are counted and stored on this PC.

3.12.1 · 2026-10-31

What's new

  • Overlay: a separate colour for each part, a switch to hide the small labels, a clock with seconds
  • All overlay designs share one font and size, on a single line
  • Internet speed unit (megabits or megabytes) in Settings, also used by the ping stability limits
  • Power use estimate that learns from every game you play
  • The last 7 days of errors open right under the health card
  • 11 new DNS servers and a coloured ping

Fixes

  • The weather animation shows for everyone
  • Switching between pages is faster and the overlay is lighter
