Shabpa / Network and ping

See what eats your internet, and stop it

When you game and an update or a torrent quietly fills your line, your ping spikes. Shabpa shows each app's internet use and lets you cap its speed or block it altogether. In-game protection does it for you automatically.

Download Shabpa

How do I keep my game ping stable?

  1. Open "Ping stability" from the side menu and watch the live ping and the stability chart.
  2. In the "what eats your internet" list, find the heavy apps.
  3. Set a download and upload cap for each (in megabits or megabytes, whichever you prefer) or block it.
  4. Turn on in-game protection so other apps are capped automatically while you play, and released when the game closes.

There is also a DNS page for switching DNS quickly, with a ping test across several servers, even behind a VPN.

Frequently asked questions

Does Shabpa lower my ping?

Shabpa does not change the route to the game server. It keeps other apps (updates, torrents, browsers) from filling your line so your ping stays stable.

Why does my antivirus sometimes warn?

For per-app speed caps Shabpa uses the open-source WinDivert driver, which some antivirus products flag because it is a network tool. If you do not need that feature, you do not have to use it.
